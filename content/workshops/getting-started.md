Welcome to the OutSystems Developer Workshops! This series is designed to take you from beginner to advanced developer on the OutSystems low-code platform.

## What You'll Learn

This workshop series covers:

1. **Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)** — Learn how to write specifications that guide development
2. **OutSystems Basics** — Master the fundamentals of the OutSystems platform
3. **Advanced Patterns** — Explore enterprise-scale architecture and best practices

## Workshop Structure

Each module is self-contained yet builds on previous knowledge. You can follow them sequentially or jump to specific topics based on your experience level.

## Getting the Most Out of These Workshops

- Complete hands-on exercises
- Work through real-world scenarios
- Engage with the community
- Practice concepts in your own projects

## Ready to Begin?

Start with the BDD Introduction or OutSystems Basics depending on your experience level.
