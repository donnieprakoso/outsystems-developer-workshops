Welcome! This is a **community-driven collection** of hands-on workshops curated by OutSystems Solutions Architects and a community of OutSystems Developers (MVPs, Champions, and contributors).

## What We're Building

All of our workshops are **hands-on** and designed to help you:

- Build **production-grade applications** with OutSystems
- Create **enterprise-ready solutions** that scale
- Explore **agentic AI** and autonomous systems with OutSystems

## A Note on This Project

This is a grassroots initiative by practitioners who believe in learning by doing. While not official (yet), we're committed to sharing practical knowledge from the field — real-world patterns, best practices, and lessons learned from building with OutSystems.

Whether you're new to OutSystems or deepening your expertise, we hope these workshops help you build better, faster, and with confidence.

**Let's learn together.** 🚀
